# ScaleQuest Story Artwork Brief

This art-brief describes the 4 panels for **The Mini-Maker Studio** story arc in ScaleQuest:

## Panel 1: The Poster Problem
- **Dimensions:** 800 × 500 px (16:10 aspect ratio), delivered as high-resolution PNG/SVG.
- **Scene:** Jack and Maya in the bright makerspace studio. Jack stands proudly next to a freshly printed exhibition poster that is noticeably stretched vertically (12 cm wide by 11 cm high). Next to it is a small photo card on the table (4 cm wide by 3 cm high).
- **Characters:**
  - Jack (cheerful makerspace builder with a tape measure) looking surprised.
  - Maya (observant maker lead holding a ruler) pointing out the unequal ratios.
  - Atlas the Ant peeking out with a tiny magnifying glass.
- **Pedagogical detail:** The poster visibly shows horizontal vs vertical distortion from adding 8 cm to both dimensions.

## Panel 2: The Magic Multiplier
- **Dimensions:** 800 × 500 px.
- **Scene:** Maya demonstrating scaling on a digital workshop board. The original 4 × 3 cm photo is enlarged smoothly by scale factor k = 3.
- **Pedagogical detail:**
  - Clear golden multiplier arrow labeled "× 3".
  - The resulting 12 cm × 9 cm poster matches the photo's aspect ratio perfectly.
  - Dashed diagonal lines showing the 5 cm diagonal expanding cleanly to 15 cm.

## Panel 3: Shrinking Back
- **Dimensions:** 800 × 500 px.
- **Scene:** Maya and Jack using a badge press to create wearable makerspace badges from the large 12 × 9 cm exhibition poster.
- **Pedagogical detail:**
  - An arrow marked "× 1/3" (or "k = 1/3") showing reduction.
  - The badge reproduces the original 4 × 3 cm photo dimensions exactly.
  - Visual note: "Back from ×3 is ×1/3 — reverse factor is 1/k".

## Panel 4: The Model Lighthouse
- **Dimensions:** 800 × 500 px.
- **Scene:** Open-day centerpiece exhibition table. A finely detailed 24 cm model lighthouse sits next to a drafted architectural blueprint showing scale 1 : 50. Through a studio window, a real harbor lighthouse can be seen.
- **Pedagogical detail:**
  - Ruler measuring the 24 cm model height.
  - The blueprint callout shows: Match ➔ Factor (k = 50) ➔ Use (24 × 50 = 1200 cm) ➔ Check (1200 cm = 12 m).
  - Atlas the Ant standing proudly on the base of the model with a tiny compass.
